Summary

Isoline is a free weather app. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. We (Mason K Studio) do not run our own servers and do not collect, store or sell your personal information.

Location

If you allow location access, Isoline reads your device's location to show weather for where you are. The coordinates are rounded to about 100 m on your device, then sent only to the services below to get the forecast and the name of your area. The app reads your location only while it is open, never in the background. If you add the home-screen widget, it refreshes the forecast about every 30 minutes using the last saved (rounded) location; it does not read your GPS in the background. You can turn this off at any time in the app ("Show my location first") or in Android settings, and add cities by searching instead.

Data stored on your device

Your saved cities, settings (theme, language, units) and the most recent weather are stored only on your phone so the app opens quickly. This data is never uploaded. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it.

Third-party services

To work, the app sends requests over encrypted connections (HTTPS) to these services. Like any website, they receive your IP address and the request contents (coordinates or the city name you search for):

We have no control over these services' own data practices; please see their policies linked above.

Children

Isoline is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
